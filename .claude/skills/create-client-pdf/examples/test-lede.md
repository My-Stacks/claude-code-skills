# Where the pricing model is leaking

*The plan is sound; the leak is in how we package it. Two tiers do the work
of three, and the middle tier is where margin and clarity both go to die.*

Everything below assumes we keep the current contract structure and only
change how tiers are presented to the buyer.

## 1 · The two-tier case

Collapsing to two tiers removes the decision paralysis that the current
middle tier creates without losing meaningful revenue.

> [!callout]
> **The resolution:** Fewer choices, sharper anchors. The buyer should be
> able to self-select in under a minute.

> [!bottom-line] Bottom line
> Two tiers, repriced anchors, no contract changes. A packaging fix, not a
> pricing overhaul.
