# The one thing to get right this weekend

Your current site is **well-written for humans and invisible to machines.** "You're not short on data — you're short on a decision you can defend" is a sharp line. The problem isn't the writing quality. It's that the words a person *searches* and an AI *answers with* don't appear anywhere on the site in a structure a machine can read.

We ran an AI-visibility report on the exact questions your buyers are putting into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI right now. Over the last 30 days, across 710 tracked AI answers:

| Firm | AI Visibility | When named, prominence |
|---|---|---|
| Bain & Company | 8% | Mid-list |
| Chief Outsiders | 6% | Dominant when listed |
| Bullish | 3% | — |
| WADE Advisory | 0% | Never surfaces |

The category leader sits at 8%. **The category is wide open.** Nobody owns it.

> [!callout]
> **The resolution, and the POV for the whole redesign:** *Mystery for humans. Clarity for machines.* The elevated, spare, cinematic treatment lives in the visual and emotional layer. A thin, deliberate layer of exact-match answer text lives underneath it.

## 1 · Website story & positioning

Pick **one** canonical public name and **one** description sentence. Use them verbatim everywhere.

> [!canonical] Canonical sentence · citable, machine-first
> "WADE Advisory is an operator-led advisory firm for consumer brand founders, retailers, commerce platforms, and capital partners navigating a category reset or capital moment: senior operator judgment on the one high-stakes call, not slide decks."

## 2 · SEO / GEO POV

We tracked 41 real prompts WADE's buyers ask AI. Use them as exact phrasings in page titles, H1s, and opening copy.

> [!bottom-line] Bottom line for the 1:30 call
> The redesign instinct (spare, elevated, mysterious) is right for humans and dangerous for machines **only if it strips the few exact-match sentences AI needs.**
>
> That's a weekend of *structured editing on the existing template*, not a rebuild.
