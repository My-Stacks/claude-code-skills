*You asked two things after the POV: how are the competitors actually doing, and which prompts should the new site be built around. Both are answered below from live data: the same engines and questions your buyers use, over the last 30 days.*

## 1 · Where the competitors actually stand

The headline isn't "WADE is behind." It's that **the boutique tier of this category is unclaimed.** Every close competitor you named (TNGE, View from 32, Third Eye Insights) plus BluWave and Mavan sits at effectively 0%, exactly where WADE sits. The only firms AI names are larger brands surfacing on brand mass, not because they answer the question better.

| Firm | Tier | AI Visibility | Share of Voice | When it appears |
|------|------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Bain & Company | Global | **8%** | 25% | Mid-list, broad questions |
| Chief Outsiders | Fractional-exec | **6%** | 57%\* | Heavy, but almost only on its *own* branded prompts |
| Bullish | Brand strategy | **3%** | 16% | Rare; ranks well when listed |
| Third Eye Insights | Boutique | **<1%** | 2% | 1 low-volume prompt, Perplexity only |
| TNGE | Boutique | **0%** | 0% | Never surfaces |
| View from 32 | Boutique | **0%** | 0% | Never surfaces |
| BluWave LP | Boutique / diligence | **0%** | 0% | Never surfaces |
| Mavan | Boutique | **0%** | 0% | Never surfaces |
| **WADE Advisory** | **Boutique** | **0%** | **0%** | **Never surfaces** |

\* *Chief Outsiders' 57% share of voice is a mechanics artifact: it dominates prompts that literally contain its name ("alternative to Chief Outsiders," "Chief Outsiders vs…"). Strip those and its real footprint is thin, with the weakest sentiment of the three (60/100 vs Bain's 68).*

**Read it three ways:**

- **Your real competitive set is at zero.** WADE is not chasing boutiques who've gotten ahead: none of them have moved. This is a land grab, not a catch-up.
- **Bain is the only true "default."** Its 8% comes from domain mass on generic questions, and it's strongest on Google AI Overview (12%), where established-domain authority compounds. It is beatable on specificity, not volume.
- **The gap is content existence, not engine choice.** The open prompts are empty across all three engines simultaneously. Peec's own opportunity scoring puts ~100% of the available gain in WADE-owned page types (service pages, articles, category pages, homepage), none of which WADE currently holds. That is precisely the POV's relocate-and-retitle move.

These are real questions buyers put to AI, ranked by volume and by how contested they are. Tier 1 is the weekend. Each maps to a page action from the POV.

**Don't chase these.** Three prompts make competitors *look* strong but are structurally unwinnable and low-volume: *"Chief Outsiders vs…,"* *"best alternative to Chief Outsiders,"* *"alternatives to Bullish."* The competitor's name is the query: that's why they win it. Ignore the scary share-of-voice number and spend the weekend on Tier 1–2.
